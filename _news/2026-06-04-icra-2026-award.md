I attended the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) in Vienna, Austria, from June 1 to 5, 2026. At the award ceremony on June 4, I received the [IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ra-l) Outstanding Associate Editor Award from the [IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS)](https://www.ieee-ras.org/).
